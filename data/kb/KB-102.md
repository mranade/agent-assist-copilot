# Complete outage checklist

Use this when the customer has no internet at all.

## Steps for the agent
1. Check the outage map on the account screen. If there is a known outage in the customer's area, give them the estimated restore time and offer text alerts. Do not schedule a technician during a known outage.
2. If no outage is listed, ask the customer to read the gateway lights (see KB-104).
3. Schedule a technician (KB-115) if the "WAN" light is solid red, which means the fiber signal is not reaching the gateway. There is no trip fee for signal problems.
4. If the lights are normal, power-cycle the gateway (KB-105) and test again.

Outages longer than 24 hours qualify for an automatic service credit (KB-109).
