# Plans and pricing

Relay Fiber offers three residential plans, all with no data caps (KB-118) and no annual contract:

- Relay 300: 300 Mbps download and upload, $50 per month.
- Relay 1 Gig: 1 Gbps download and upload, $70 per month.
- Relay 2 Gig: 2 Gbps download and upload, $95 per month. Includes a Wi-Fi 7 gateway.

All plans include the gateway at no extra charge.
