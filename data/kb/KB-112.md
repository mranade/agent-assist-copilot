# Moving service to a new address

Customers who are moving can transfer their service to a new address if Relay Fiber is available there. Check availability with the address lookup on the account screen.

- Ask for at least 14 days' notice so an installation can be scheduled.
- The $99 installation fee is waived for existing customers who transfer service.
- The customer keeps their current plan and price at the new address.
- If Relay Fiber is not available at the new address, the move is handled as a cancellation (KB-113) with no early-termination fee.

## Steps for the agent
1. Ask for the new address. Check availability with the address lookup on the account screen.
2. Ask for the move date. Schedule the installation with at least 14 days' notice.
3. Tell them the $99 installation fee is waived for existing customers who transfer service.
4. Confirm that they keep their current plan and price at the new address.
