# Gateway light meanings

The Relay Fiber gateway has three lights: Power, WAN, and Wi-Fi.

- Power solid white: normal. Off: check the power adapter and outlet.
- WAN solid white: connected to the fiber network. Blinking white: connecting, wait two minutes. Solid red: no fiber signal, schedule a technician (KB-115).
- Wi-Fi solid blue: Wi-Fi is broadcasting. Off: Wi-Fi has been disabled in the Relay app; turn it back on under Settings > Wi-Fi.
