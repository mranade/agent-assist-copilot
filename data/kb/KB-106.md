# Changing the Wi-Fi name or password

Customers can change their Wi-Fi name and password in the Relay app under Settings > Wi-Fi, or at the gateway admin page. Agents can also push a new password from the account screen after verifying the customer (KB-117). Devices will need to reconnect with the new password. Passwords must be at least 12 characters.
