# Understanding your bill

The monthly bill shows the plan charge, any add-ons (such as a static IP, KB-119), discounts (such as autopay, KB-116), and taxes and fees.

When a customer changes plans or starts service partway through a billing cycle, the first bill includes a prorated charge for the partial month. This is the most common reason a bill looks higher than expected. Walk the customer through the "Prorated charges" section line by line.
