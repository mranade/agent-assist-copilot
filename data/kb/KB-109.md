# Service credits for outages and repeated drops

## Outages over 24 hours
Customers receive an automatic credit for any outage longer than 24 hours. The credit is the daily rate of their plan (the monthly plan price divided by 30) for each full day of the outage.

## Repeated intermittent drops
For repeated drops that were reported and confirmed by a line check or technician, agents may apply a one-time goodwill credit of up to $20 without supervisor approval. Credits above $20 need supervisor approval (KB-120).

Credits appear on the next bill. Tell the customer the amount and when they will see it.
