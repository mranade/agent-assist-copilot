# Technician appointments

Technician visits are scheduled in 2-hour windows from 8 am to 8 pm, Monday through Saturday. The earliest available window is usually within 2 business days.

- No charge when the problem is with Relay Fiber's line or equipment.
- A $75 trip fee applies when the technician finds the problem is the customer's own inside wiring or personal devices. Always tell the customer about this possibility before booking.

The customer gets a text 30 minutes before the technician arrives.

## Steps for the agent
1. Tell them that a $75 trip fee applies if the technician finds the problem is their own inside wiring or personal devices, and there is no charge when the problem is with Relay Fiber's line or equipment.
2. Book the earliest 2-hour window that works for them, between 8 am and 8 pm, Monday through Saturday.
3. Tell them they will get a text 30 minutes before the technician arrives.
