# Cancellation and retention offers

Before processing a cancellation, ask why the customer is leaving and try to solve the underlying problem.

## Steps for the agent
1. Ask why they are leaving. Try to solve the underlying problem first.
2. Offer a retention option: 20% off their plan for 3 months if they cite price and have more than 12 months of tenure, or a technician visit (KB-115) if they are leaving because of service issues.
3. If they still want to cancel, explain that service ends at the end of the current billing cycle and there is no early-termination fee.
4. Email them a prepaid return label (KB-114). Remind them to return the gateway within 30 days to avoid the $150 unreturned-equipment fee.

## Retention offers
- Customers with more than 12 months of tenure who cite price may be offered 20% off their plan for 3 months. Agents can apply this once per account without approval.
- Customers leaving because of service issues should be offered a technician visit (KB-115) first.

## Processing a cancellation
Service ends at the end of the current billing cycle. There is no early-termination fee. The customer must return the gateway within 30 days (KB-114), or a $150 unreturned-equipment fee is charged.
