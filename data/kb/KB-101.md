# Intermittent connection drops

Use this when a customer says their internet "keeps dropping", "cuts out", or disconnects for a few seconds or minutes at a time.

## Likely causes
- Evening neighborhood congestion (drops cluster between 7 pm and 11 pm).
- Wi-Fi interference, especially on the 2.4 GHz band in apartment buildings.
- An overheating or outdated gateway.

## Steps for the agent
1. Run a line check from the account screen. If the line check shows errors, skip to step 5.
2. Ask whether drops happen on wired devices too. Wired drops point to the line or gateway, Wi-Fi-only drops point to interference.
3. For Wi-Fi-only drops, have the customer switch devices to the 5 GHz network (the network name ending in "-5G") and move the gateway off the floor and away from microwaves.
4. Power-cycle the gateway (see KB-105) and confirm the firmware updated automatically.
5. If drops continue after these steps, schedule a technician visit (see KB-115). Log the ticket as "Intermittent - repeat".

## Credits
If the customer has had repeated drops over several days, they may qualify for a service credit. See KB-109.
