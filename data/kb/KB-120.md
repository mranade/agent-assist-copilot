# Escalation and supervisor transfers

Transfer to a supervisor when:
- The customer requests a credit or waiver beyond the agent's approval limits (KB-108, KB-109).
- The customer explicitly asks for a supervisor after the agent has tried to resolve the issue.
- The customer raises a legal or safety concern.

For questions about products Relay Fiber does not offer, tell the customer plainly that it isn't offered rather than guessing.
Before transferring, add a short note to the account summarizing the issue so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.
