# Upgrading or downgrading a plan

Customers can change plans at any time with no change fee. Upgrades take effect immediately and the difference is prorated on the next bill (KB-107). Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Moving to Relay 2 Gig requires swapping to the Wi-Fi 7 gateway, which ships free in two business days.
