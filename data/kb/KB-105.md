# Power-cycling and factory reset

## Power-cycle (try this first)
Unplug the gateway's power cable, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. Wait about three minutes for the WAN light to turn solid white. A power-cycle keeps all of the customer's settings.

## Factory reset (last resort)
Hold the recessed reset button on the back for 15 seconds with a paperclip. A factory reset erases the custom Wi-Fi network name and password, so warn the customer before they do it. After a reset, the Wi-Fi name and password go back to the ones printed on the gateway's label.
